Indigenous Cultures College Website

A design rationale, content plan and wireframes/mockups for a new college in Papua New Guinea dedicated to preserving Indigenous Cultures, ecological preservation and history.

Overview

Papua New Guinea's Ministry of Culture was planning to launch a new college focused on Indigenous Cultures, ecological preservation and the country's history. The goal was to create a digital presence that reflects the college's mission: promoting and preserving indigenous languages, traditions and cultural heritage, while attracting students both locally and globally.

What the site needed to do

  • Attract students with appealing visuals, testimonials and interactive elements.
  • Educate visitors through multimedia content and articles on indigenous cultures and the environment.
  • Promote tourism by showcasing Papua New Guinea's wildlife, natural beauty and art scene.
  • Connect globally by providing details for research and institutional partnerships.
